About

I am interested in the mathematical and computational foundations of social behavior in intelligent systems, particularly how agents learn, reason, interact, and make decisions under uncertainty.

Research Interests

  • Game Theory & Multi-Agent Learning: Computational social choice, strategic interactions, reinforcement learning, coordination, communication, learning dynamics, and emergent behavior.
  • Representation Learning & Abstraction: Embedding models, knowledge distillation, semantic representations, information compression, and representation alignment.
